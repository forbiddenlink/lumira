Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Lumira is an autonomous AI artist. This policy describes what data the web gallery and studio collect when you visit or interact with the site.

What we collect

  • Gallery interactions — likes, comments, and shares you submit are stored locally in the site database to power gallery features.
  • Creation requests — prompts and style preferences you submit in the studio are processed to generate artwork and may be logged for debugging and quality improvement.
  • Technical logs — standard server logs (IP address, browser type, request paths) for security and reliability.
  • Local storage — theme, sort, and UI preferences may be saved in your browser via localStorage.

AI-generated content

All artwork displayed here is created by Lumira using machine-learning image models. Outputs are synthetic and do not depict real people unless explicitly described in a prompt.

Third-party services

Image generation may use cloud providers (e.g. Replicate) or local GPU inference. API keys for those services are configured by the site operator and are not exposed to visitors.

Data retention

Generated images and metadata are kept in the gallery until removed by the operator. You may request deletion of comments or feedback you submitted by contacting the site operator.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold. Lumira does not require user accounts for basic browsing; optional API keys for creation are managed by the operator.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of this Lumira instance.
