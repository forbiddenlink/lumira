## Weakness map

- W1. The work owns the room. Evidence: Zwirner installation photo (03), Met object (07), Emmerer plate (05), Goodman exhibition image (11).
- W2. Presence as a sentence, not meters. Evidence: Rauno's opening sentence (09). No reference showed a mood as energy and intensity percentages. The visitor line should be written, and the numbers stay in the studio instruments.
- W3. One primary action. Evidence: Artsy Purchase with Make an Offer as the second button (10), Goodman "Request a preview" (11), Botto's single "Visit Studio" (01). None of these is an artist studio with two create rows, so collapsing Lumira's two create systems into one filled action and one quieter commission is an original decision.
- W4. Human titles. Evidence: Zwirner "Coffee, 2026" (03), Met "Wheat Field with Cypresses" (07), Obvious series names and years (06), Elektra titles plus a name (08), Artsy "Ciel d'automne, 2021" (10), Goodman "A Nicely Built City" (11).
- W5. Do not cover the work. Evidence: Are.na's bottom bar leaves the error text clear (12). Artsy's phone tooltip and app banner crushed the painting (10). Goodman's cookie card sat on the image (11). Lumira's floating Ask button should leave the picture.
- W6. Phone navigation that fits. Evidence: Obvious Physical/Digital switches (06), Are.na's bottom bar (02, 12), Elektra's collapsed header (08). A non-scrolling tab row is the failure Emmerer also showed when a four-column record became a thin strip (05).
- W7. One header. Evidence: Botto's compact header (01), Goodman wordmark plus Menu (11), Are.na's header on both the home and the error (02, 12).
- W8. A sentence and a way back. Evidence: Are.na "Page not found" with Home and Explore (12). Artsy's missing work had the sentence and no next step on the first screen (10), which is the part not to copy.
- W9. One main action, the rest quieter. Evidence: Met's icon row under the picture and a separate Artwork Details panel, with Technical Notes tested (07). Artsy's filled Purchase and outline Make an Offer (10).
